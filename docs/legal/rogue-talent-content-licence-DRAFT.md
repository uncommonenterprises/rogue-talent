# Rogue Talent — Content Licence & Model Release  ·  **DRAFT (for solicitor review)**

> ⚠️ **DRAFT — not legal advice.** Written by the Rogue Talent team as a starting point; **must be
> reviewed and finalised by a qualified UK solicitor before use.** Solicitor focus points are listed at
> the end. One standard agreement applies to **every** booking — there is no per-booking usage
> negotiation (deliberately; see `docs/spikes/contracts-usage-rights.md` and Neil's 2026-09-21 brief).

Governs the content created when a Client books a Model through Rogue Talent. Formed when the **Client
accepts these terms at checkout** and the **Model accepts the booking** — both recorded on the platform.

---

## The key terms, in plain English (this box is a summary; the clauses below are what's binding)
- **You (the Client) can use the photos and video from this shoot however you reasonably want — for
  any commercial or marketing purpose, on any media, worldwide, forever.** No usage period, no
  re-licensing fees, no coming back to re-clear.
- **The booking fee is the whole cost.** There is no separate or future "usage fee".
- **You can edit, crop and adapt the content, and let your own clients/partners use it** for the same purposes.
- **A few common-sense limits protect the Model** — you can't use the content unlawfully, in a
  defamatory or pornographic way, or to falsely imply the Model endorses certain sensitive things.
- **The Model just does the shoot and gets paid** — no ongoing rights to manage, no expiry to track.

## Agreement

**Schedule (auto-filled per booking):** Client name/company · Model name · Shoot date(s) · Location ·
Booking reference · Fee. *(Rogue Talent inserts these from the booking.)*

**1. Definitions.** "**Content**" means all photographs, video and other material featuring the Model
created during the booked shoot. "**Client**" and "**Model**" are the parties named in the Schedule.
"**Platform**" means Rogue Talent.

**2. Licence granted.** The Model grants the Client a **worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable,
non-exclusive, royalty-free, sub-licensable licence** to use, reproduce, edit, adapt, crop, distribute,
publish and display the Content for any lawful **commercial, advertising, marketing, editorial and
internal purpose, across all media now known or later devised** (including websites, social media, paid
/ performance media, print, broadcast, out-of-home and packaging). The Client may permit its group
companies, clients and production partners to use the Content for the same purposes.

**3. Consideration — one fee, no usage charges.** The booking fee stated in the Schedule is the Model's
**full and only** payment for the shoot **and** this licence. **No further, recurring or usage-based
fees** are or will be payable, and the licence **does not expire**.

**4. Model release & likeness.** The Model consents to the use of their **image, likeness and
performance** as part of the Content on the terms above, and, to the fullest extent permitted by law,
**waives any moral rights** in the Content. The Model's **name is not used to imply endorsement** except
as permitted in clause 5.

**5. Protections (limits on use).** The Client will **not** use the Content: (a) unlawfully, or in
breach of any third-party rights; (b) in a **defamatory, obscene or pornographic** manner, or in a way
that a reasonable person would find seriously demeaning to the Model; (c) so as to imply the Model's
**personal endorsement** of political parties or candidates, adult services, tobacco/vaping, firearms,
or gambling, **without the Model's separate written consent**; or (d) materially altered so as to
present the Model in a **false or misleading** light. *(Solicitor to confirm/adjust this list.)*

**6. Model warranties.** The Model confirms they are **18 or over**, are **free to grant this licence**,
and are under no conflicting obligation that would prevent it.

**7. Client warranties.** The Client will use the Content **lawfully** and holds all rights needed for
any brands, products, sets or third-party materials appearing in the shoot.

**8. No exclusivity.** Nothing here restricts the Model from working with anyone else.

**9. Platform's role.** Rogue Talent **facilitates** the booking and payment and provides this standard
agreement; the licence is **between the Client and the Model**. Rogue Talent is not a party to the
creative work. *(Solicitor: confirm this sits correctly with the agent/principal position — see
`docs/compliance-open-items.md` §2.)*

**10. Liability.** Each party's liability under this agreement is limited to the booking fee, save for
liability that cannot be excluded by law. *(Placeholder — solicitor to set the proper liability wording.)*

**11. Governing law.** England & Wales; the courts of England & Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.

**12. Acceptance.** The Client accepts these terms by ticking the acceptance box at checkout; the Model
accepts by accepting the booking. Rogue Talent records the date, parties and agreement version.

## For the solicitor — please review / confirm
1. **Enforceability of the moral-rights waiver (cl. 4)** under UK law (CDPA 1988) and whether a
   "consent not to assert" is needed instead.
2. **The protections list (cl. 5)** — is it the right minimum set; anything to add/remove for a
   perpetual broad licence to be fair + defensible?
3. **Clickwrap sufficiency** — is recorded click-acceptance (Client at checkout, Model at booking
   acceptance) sufficient to form this as a binding contract for v1, or is more needed?
4. **Agent vs principal (cl. 9)** — align with the VAT/commercial position (compliance-open-items §2).
5. **Liability (cl. 10)** and any **consumer-law** considerations (some clients may be sole traders/individuals).
6. **Data protection** — likeness is personal data; confirm this + the Privacy Policy cover it.
7. Overall: is this **as short as it can be while remaining binding + comprehensive**? (Neil's explicit goal.)
