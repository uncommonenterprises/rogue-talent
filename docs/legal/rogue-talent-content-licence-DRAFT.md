Governs the content created when a Client books a Model through Rogue Talent. Formed when the **Client
accepts these terms at checkout** and the **Model accepts the booking** — both recorded on the platform.

## The key terms, in plain English (this box is a summary; the clauses below are what's binding)
- **You (the Client) can use the photos and video from this shoot however you reasonably want — for
  any commercial or marketing purpose, on any media, worldwide, forever.** No usage period, no
  re-licensing fees, no coming back to re-clear.
- **The booking fee is the whole cost.** There is no separate or future "usage fee".
- **You can edit, crop and adapt the content freely**, and use your own agency/printer/platform to put it out **on your behalf**.
- **The content is yours to use, but not to sell on.** You can't transfer, re-sell or hand the images
  to another company for *their* use — the licence is for *you*, the booker.
- **A few common-sense limits protect the Model** — you can't use the content unlawfully, in a
  defamatory or pornographic way, or to falsely imply the Model endorses certain sensitive things.
- **No AI training or synthetic "deepfake" likenesses** — you can't feed the content into AI models or
  generate synthetic versions of the Model without their separate consent.
- **If you break these limits, the Model can pull the licence** (and ask for the content to be taken
  down) — otherwise it runs forever. Using the content normally is never affected.
- **The Model just does the shoot and gets paid** — no ongoing rights to manage, no expiry to track —
  though they can still show the images in their own portfolio and be credited.

## Agreement

**Schedule (auto-filled per booking):** Client name/company · Model name · Shoot date(s) · Location ·
Booking reference · Fee. *(Rogue Talent inserts these from the booking.)*

**1. Definitions.** "**Content**" means all photographs, video and other material featuring the Model
created during the booked shoot. "**Client**" and "**Model**" are the parties named in the Schedule.
"**Platform**" means Rogue Talent.

**2. Licence granted.** The Model grants the Client a **worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable (subject to
clause 5B), non-exclusive, royalty-free licence** to use, reproduce, edit, adapt, crop, distribute,
publish and display the Content for any lawful **commercial, advertising, marketing, editorial and
internal purpose, across all media now known or later devised** (including websites, social media, paid
/ performance media, print, broadcast, out-of-home and packaging).

**2A. For the Client only — no transfer or re-sale (model protection).** This licence is granted to the
**Client named in the Schedule for the Client's own use** only. The Client may **not sell, transfer,
assign, or sub-license the Content** to any third party for that third party's own use. The Client
**may** allow its own service providers (e.g. its agency, printer, hosting or media platform) to handle
the Content **on the Client's behalf, solely for the Client's own permitted use** — this is not a
transfer or re-sale. The Model **retains the right to use the Content in their own portfolio,
show-reel and self-promotion**, and may **ask to be credited** where reasonably practical (a credit is
requested, not required of the Client). *(Solicitor to word the "on the Client's behalf" carve-out precisely.)*

**3. Consideration — one fee, no usage charges.** The booking fee stated in the Schedule is the Model's
**full and only** payment for the shoot **and** this licence. **No further, recurring or usage-based
fees** are or will be payable, and the licence **does not expire**. The Model **acknowledges that this
fee is agreed as full and fair consideration for a perpetual, all-media licence** (a "full buyout"),
that they understand the licence is broad and lasting, and that they enter into it freely.

**4. Model release & likeness.** The Model consents to the use of their **image, likeness and
performance** as part of the Content on the terms above, and, to the fullest extent permitted by law,
**waives any moral rights** in the Content. The Model's **name is not used to imply endorsement** except
as permitted in clause 5.

**5. Protections (limits on use).** The Client will **not** use the Content: (a) unlawfully, or in
breach of any third-party rights; (b) in a **defamatory, obscene or pornographic** manner, or in a way
that a reasonable person would find seriously demeaning to the Model; (c) so as to imply the Model's
**personal endorsement** of political parties or candidates, adult services, tobacco/vaping, firearms,
or gambling, **without the Model's separate written consent**; or (d) materially altered so as to
present the Model in a **false or misleading** light. *(Solicitor to confirm/adjust this list.)*

**5A. AI, machine learning & synthetic likeness (model protection).** The Client will **not**, without the
Model's **separate, specific written consent**: (a) use the Content to **train, fine-tune or develop any
artificial-intelligence or machine-learning model**; or (b) use the Content to **create synthetic,
AI-generated or "deepfake" imagery, video or audio of the Model**, or a digital replica of the Model's
likeness or voice. Ordinary editing, retouching and adaptation of the actual Content under clause 2
remain permitted. *(Solicitor to confirm scope + wording; this is a fast-moving area — see benchmark
research.)*

**5B. Revocation on material breach + takedown.** The licence in clause 2 is irrevocable **except** that,
if the Client **materially breaches** clauses 2A (transfer/re-sale), 5 (limits on use) or 5A (AI), and
does **not remedy** the breach within a reasonable period after written notice, the Model may **revoke the
licence in respect of the infringing use** and require the Client to **stop that use and take down the
affected Content**. Revocation applies only to the breaching use; the Client's other lawful, compliant
uses continue. *(Solicitor to set the notice period, the "material breach" threshold, and whether
revocation is partial or total.)*

**6. Model warranties.** The Model confirms they are **18 or over**, are **free to grant this licence**,
and are under no conflicting obligation that would prevent it.

**7. Client warranties.** The Client will use the Content **lawfully** and holds all rights needed for
any brands, products, sets or third-party materials appearing in the shoot.

**8. No exclusivity.** Nothing here restricts the Model from working with anyone else.

**9. Platform's role (kept deliberately light).** This booking is made and paid for through the Rogue
Talent platform. Rogue Talent **facilitates** the booking and payment only; **this licence is between
the Client and the Model, and Rogue Talent is not a party to it.** Rogue Talent's own role,
obligations and responsibilities (payments, holding funds, disputes, approvals, safety) are governed by
the separate **Rogue Talent Terms of Service** that each user agrees to — not by this licence.
*(Solicitor: confirm this licence-vs-ToS split, that keeping Rogue's responsibilities OUT of the
licence appropriately limits its liability, and that it sits correctly with the agent/principal
position — `docs/compliance-open-items.md` §2.)*

**10. Liability.** Each party's liability under this agreement is limited to the booking fee, save for
liability that cannot be excluded by law. *(Placeholder — solicitor to set the proper liability wording.)*

**11. Governing law.** England & Wales; the courts of England & Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.

**12. Acceptance.** The Client accepts these terms by ticking the acceptance box at checkout; the Model
accepts by accepting the booking. Rogue Talent records the date, parties and agreement version.

## For the solicitor — please review / confirm
1. **Enforceability of the moral-rights waiver (cl. 4)** under UK law (CDPA 1988) and whether a
   "consent not to assert" is needed instead.
2. **The protections list (cl. 5)** — is it the right minimum set; anything to add/remove for a
   perpetual broad licence to be fair + defensible?
2A. **The no-transfer/no-re-sale + "on the Client's behalf" carve-out (cl. 2A)** — word it so the
   Model is protected from their image being sold on or independently exploited by third parties, while
   the Client's own service providers (agency/printer/platform) can still deliver the Client's own
   permitted use. Where exactly is the line?
2B. **Licence vs Terms of Service split (cl. 9)** — confirm Rogue Talent's responsibilities belong in
   the separate ToS (not this licence) and that this limits Rogue's liability appropriately.
3. **Clickwrap sufficiency** — is recorded click-acceptance (Client at checkout, Model at booking
   acceptance) sufficient to form this as a binding contract for v1, or is more needed?
4. **Agent vs principal (cl. 9)** — align with the VAT/commercial position (compliance-open-items §2).
5. **Liability (cl. 10)** and any **consumer-law** considerations (some clients may be sole traders/individuals).
6. **Data protection** — likeness is personal data; confirm this + the Privacy Policy cover it.
7. Overall: is this **as short as it can be while remaining binding + comprehensive**? (Neil's explicit goal.)
8. **AI / synthetic-likeness carve-out (cl. 5A)** — is the drafting robust and future-proof for AI
   training and deepfakes, and is a blanket prohibition-without-consent the right default (vs. narrower)?
9. **Revocability on breach (cl. 5B)** — does the "irrevocable except on material breach + takedown"
   construction hold up in E&W law? Set the notice/cure period and the material-breach threshold, and
   confirm partial (breaching-use-only) revocation is workable.
10. **Consumer fairness of a perpetual single-fee "full buyout" (cl. 2, 3)** — where the Model may be a
   **consumer or sole trader**, is a perpetual, irrevocable, all-media licence for one fee fair and
   enforceable under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 (unfair-terms), or is a cooling-off / cap / plain-language
   safeguard needed? (Benchmark: full buyouts exist in UK agency practice but usually at a premium.)
11. **Category / competitor conflict** — should the standard licence let a Model **decline specific
   sensitive categories or direct-competitor use**, or is the cl. 5 endorsement carve-out enough for v1?
   (Post-launch model-toggle is the planned home for finer control — is anything needed at launch?)
