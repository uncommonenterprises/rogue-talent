What happens next

Our team reviews every profile and portfolio by hand before it goes live. We'll email you as soon as there's a decision. You can verify your identity and bank details with Stripe at any time before or after this, and it won't slow your review down. You're available by default from the moment you go live; every booking request still needs your acceptance, so there's nothing to set up here.

Design notes
  • Every section has its own "Edit" link back to that wizard step. No single monolithic "Edit profile" button, so a model correcting one thing (say, a rate) isn't sent back through the whole flow.
  • "Submit for approval" replaces the old Stripe-gated "Publish listing" wording. Per the account-status-lifecycle spec, Gate B (Stripe) is explicitly not a prerequisite to submit for Gate A (quality review).
  • The "what happens next" callout makes no promise about review turnaround time. We don't have data to back a number yet, and RT-FB-09 already flagged the cost of publishing unbacked claims.
  • A single ownership/accuracy tick box sits just above the submit button, lightweight, but gives the model an explicit accountability moment for the portfolio images specifically (SAF-02 territory).
  • RT-FB-10 (Neil, 28/09): there is no Availability section on this review screen any more, and the stepper above is 4 steps, not 6. Models are available by default; every booking request still needs acceptance within a 48-hour window, and an accepted booking auto-blocks those dates, so there's no onboarding step needed to set this up. Models can optionally block out dates later from their dashboard: see the "Manage your calendar" link on screens 14 and 16.
