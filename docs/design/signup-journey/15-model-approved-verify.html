Your profile passed review. One step left before clients can find and book you.

Why we ask

Every model on Rogue Talent is identity and bank verified before they're visible. It's what lets a client book and pay you directly with confidence, and it's the same standard we hold every client to.

  • A photo ID (passport or driving licence)
  • A quick live selfie, to match your ID
  • Bank details, so we can pay you direct

Takes a few minutes. You'll be taken to Stripe, our verification and payments partner, then brought straight back here.

Design notes
  • The "why we ask" framing is safety-first and honest to what actually happens in v1 (SAF-01: Stripe Identity/KYC, not a wider promise). It doesn't reach for a bigger safety claim than the product delivers.
  • One primary cobalt action: "Verify with Stripe". It explicitly says the user will leave the site, so arriving back isn't disorienting (matches Stripe/Plaid-style handoff conventions).
  • The three requirements are listed before the button, not after, so nobody taps through cold and abandons Stripe's own form when they realise they need their bank details.
