Verify your identity to start booking

Northside Studio is approved. One step left before you can send your first booking request.

Why we ask

For the safety of the models on Rogue Talent, we ask every client to verify their identity before their first booking. The same standard we hold every model to before they're visible. It's a one-off check, not a recurring one.

  • A photo ID (passport or driving licence)
  • A quick live selfie, to match your ID

Takes a few minutes. You'll be taken to Stripe Identity, then brought straight back here.

Design notes
  • Mirrors the model's Approved-verify screen (15) for pattern consistency, but the "why we ask" framing is deliberately different: for clients, verification is explicitly framed as being for the models' safety, not the client's own benefit. That's the honest reason (SAF-03) and it's a more persuasive one than a generic "verify your account" line.
  • Only two requirements listed (no bank details needed for a client, that's a model-side ask), so this card is shorter than screen 15's.
  • "Browse models" stays available as a secondary action, consistent with the Pending screen. Browsing was never gated, only booking is.
