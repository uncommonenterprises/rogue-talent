Pattern: confirmation on arrival

Verified isn't a page: it's a banner on the page you land on

Resolves RT-FB-07/RT-FB-08. Rather than a standalone "you're verified" interstitial (one more click, one more dead end), a dismissible success banner appears once on whatever the user's correct next destination already is. It's a common pattern for email confirmation in well-run products: it tells you what happened without making you leave the thing you were about to do.

Design notes
  • A banner-on-destination beats a standalone "verified" page because it never strands the user one click short of the thing they actually came to do, and it still gives an explicit, visible confirmation (the silent redirect Neil flagged in RT-FB-08 is gone either way).
  • Banner is green (success), dismissible, and shows once, not as a persistent fixture, so it doesn't clutter return visits.
  • State B's pre-filled, password-only login is the smallest possible ask given the platform constraint (no magic-link auth). It reframes "you're logged out" as a one-field form, not a dead end.
  • Client destination changes from the marketing homepage to Browse models (RT-FB-08); the homepage's signed-out "Create your account" CTA should also stop showing once logged in. Flagged for the Developer, not just this page.
