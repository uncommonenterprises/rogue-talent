Design notes
  • No stock photography anywhere in this file. Every image slot uses the DS diagonal-stripe placeholder with a "Model photography" label, so it's unmistakably a placeholder for real portfolio shots, not a finished screen.
  • Both states are shown stacked (empty and filled) per the brief, each with its own Continue button state; in the live product only one shows at a time depending on upload count.
  • The first uploaded photo carries an "Ink" badge marked "Cover". Since Gate A review is specifically a portfolio-quality check (account-status-lifecycle §2), it's worth being explicit about which shot leads on the live card.
  • Decided by Neil, 29/09: a minimum of 3 photos is required before "Continue" is enabled. The empty state shows a "0 of 3 minimum" counter next to a disabled Continue button and a hint below the upload tile; once 3 or more photos are uploaded (five shown in the filled state above), Continue is enabled.
  • Wizard order (PM decision, 29/09): portfolio is now the last of the 4 wizard steps (previously step 3, now step 4), matching the live product. "Continue" here goes to Review & submit; the step before it is Your rates (screen 11), not Your profile.
  • Mobile pass (29/09/2026): the empty state's footer row (Back, plus a "0 of 3 minimum" hint next to the disabled Continue button) was tight at 375px. .wizard-actions now allows wrapping (flex-wrap + a row-gap) so, if it ever runs out of room on a narrow phone, it breaks cleanly onto two lines instead of crowding - no visible change at any width where it already fits, including desktop.
