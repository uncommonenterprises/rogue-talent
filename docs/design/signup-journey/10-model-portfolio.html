Design notes
  • No stock photography anywhere in this file. Every image slot uses the DS diagonal-stripe placeholder with a "Model photography" label, so it's unmistakably a placeholder for real portfolio shots, not a finished screen.
  • Both states are shown stacked (empty and filled) per the brief; in the live product only one shows at a time depending on upload count.
  • The first uploaded photo carries an "Ink" badge marked "Cover". Since Gate A review is specifically a portfolio-quality check (account-status-lifecycle §2), it's worth being explicit about which shot leads on the live card.
  • Open question: there's no minimum-photo requirement enforced anywhere today, but Gate A exists specifically to review portfolio quality. Worth deciding whether "Continue"/"Submit for approval" should require a minimum (e.g. 3 photos) before it's enabled.
