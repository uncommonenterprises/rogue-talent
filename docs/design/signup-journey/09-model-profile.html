Design notes
  • This is a representative subset of the real field list (configListing.js). Every required attribute is shown at least once, grouped into four labelled sections (Basics, Measurements, Style & experience, Where you'll work) rather than one long undifferentiated list, so the page reads as a form, not a survey.
  • Section headings use the tracked-uppercase label style (`rt-eyebrow`-style), matching the DS's "labels are tracked Hanken" rule, kept distinct from field labels so the hierarchy is unambiguous at a glance.
  • Multi-select fields (ethnicity, categories) use `rt-chip` toggle groups rather than a scrolling checkbox list or a multi-select dropdown. Faster to scan and matches the existing chip pattern used elsewhere in the product.
  • Optional fields (Website, Instagram) are explicitly labelled "(optional)" per the already-shipped app-wide convention in `CustomExtendedDataField.js`.
  • "Back" is a secondary button (not a link) since this is a long step and users may want a clear, deliberate way to return, but it stays visually subordinate to the one cobalt "Continue".
  • "Availability radius" here is unrelated to the calendar-availability step that's been removed (RT-FB-10). It's a travel-distance preference, stays part of the profile attributes.
  • Wizard order (PM decision, 29/09): "Continue" now goes to Your rates (screen 11), not Your portfolio. Portfolio is the new last step, before Review & submit.
