Your rates

Set your rates. Your day rate is used for bookings, and you can add optional half-day and hourly rates too. Clients book and pay you directly, with no agency taking a cut, and you can update your rates whenever you like.

How the fee works

Clients pay one flat 15% booking fee on top of your rate. You keep 100% of the £450 shown above, there's no commission taken from your side.

Design notes
  • Currency inputs use a plain "£" prefix inside the field rather than a separate currency selector. The marketplace is GBP-only, so a picker would be a false choice.
  • The fee-explainer callout is the one place on this step that states the commercial model plainly (100% to the model, one flat 15% fee to the client). It's the wedge, so it earns a moment of emphasis without becoming a second cobalt element (the callout uses a neutral surface, not accent fill).
  • Guidance copy reused verbatim from the live EditListingPricingPanel.guidance string.
  • New field (PM decision, 29/09): Minimum booking notice. A required rt-select, example value "48 hours". This sets an expectation only, it does not claim the system blocks or filters booking requests that fall inside the window, because it doesn't: the model can still decline any request regardless of notice given. The hint says so explicitly.
  • Revision 3 (Neil, 29/09) - How far you'll travel: moved here from screen 09's old "Where you'll work" section, and relabelled from "Availability radius" for clarity. Required, sits directly below Travel costs since both are travel-related (stacked rather than side by side, per Neil 29/09, because one is a pill group and one a dropdown). Options: "Local only (25 miles)", "Regional (50 miles)", "National", "International"; example value shown is "National". Underlying field is unchanged - it's still availability_radius in Sharetribe. Only the label and its position in the wizard change; this is a Console label edit at build time, not a new field.
  • Wizard order (PM decision, 29/09): Rates is step 3 of 4 now, matching the live product. "Continue" goes to Your portfolio (screen 10), the new last step, not straight to Review & submit. There is still no separate availability step; models are available by default and every booking request still needs their acceptance.
