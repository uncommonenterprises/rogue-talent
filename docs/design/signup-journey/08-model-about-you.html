About you

We've set your display name to your first name and last initial. This is how clients see you, so your full surname stays private. Change it if you model under a professional name. Then confirm the city you're based in. You'll add your stats and details on the next step.

Shown publicly on your profile instead of your full name.
Search suggestions appear as you type (Google Places). You control how precisely your location is shown to clients.

  • Guidance copy is reused verbatim from the live EditListingProfilePanel.guidance string. It already reads well and matches the brand voice.
