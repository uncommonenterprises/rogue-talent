Design notes
  • Fixes finding 11 (the big solid-blue calendar). No solid cobalt block anywhere in the calendar. The first and last day are ink-900 with white text (the same treatment as a selected rt-chip), the days between are a faint accent-50 band, today is a small dot under the number, and the only cobalt on screen is the one primary button. Hover is ink-100. Focus is a 2px cobalt inset ring.
  • Unavailable days are ink-300 with a hairline strikethrough, so they read as "not available" without relying on colour alone and without using red (red is danger only). Blocked-by-the-model and already-booked look identical on purpose. Days before today are ink-300 with no strike.
  • The end date counts as a full day. The summary box says so on every range ("2 full days. Your end date counts as a full day."), and the price breakdown uses the same day count (B05). Tapping one day twice gives a single full day.
  • Phone: a scrolling stack of four months, not a paged single month. Seven columns across 343px gives 49px day cells, so every date is a comfortable tap target, and scrolling is easier than hunting for a tiny next-month arrow. The sheet footer holds the single cobalt "Apply dates". Desktop: one month with 44px arrows inside a popover.
  • Week starts on Monday, as in the UK and as in the model's own calendar spec. Dates are written "Wed 25 Nov" everywhere.
  • Engineering: this replaces the template's react-dates styling (BookingDatesForm); the behaviour (a range, unavailable days from the model's calendar and bookings) is unchanged. The notice line needs the model's min_booking_notice and the current time; it is informational only.
