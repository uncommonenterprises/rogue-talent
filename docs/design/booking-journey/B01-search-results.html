Design notes
  • Phone fix (run-through M5). Below 900px the grid is 2-up with a compact card: photo, city, name, day rate and one meta line. Tags, the hairline rule and the "View" button are hidden, and the whole card is the tap target. At 375px two rows fit on one screen, where the old full card showed one model per screen. From 600px it is 3-up (still compact); from 900px the sidebar appears and the full rt-talent card returns, 2-up then 3-up from 1180px.
  • The Verified badge stays retired from cards. The lifecycle spec (account-status-lifecycle section 9) retired it because every visible model is Verified, so a badge on every card says nothing and implies some models are not. Replacement: one line under the heading (the trust line) stating the standard once, in wording already used on the approved client "ready to book" screen. See open question 1 on the index.
  • Search criteria that matter most are always visible. City and dates sit above the results on every screen size, not inside the filter panel. Dates use the same range calendar as the booking panel (B04), with the end date counting as a full day. The green availability dot only appears on a card when a date range is set, because without dates "free" would be meaningless: models are available by default.
  • Filters. Sidebar on desktop, full-height sheet on phones (B02). The "Filters" button shows the number of active filter values; active filters repeat as removable chips under the results count so a client can always see why a list is short. Sorting follows the configured options (Newest, Lowest price, Highest price). "Oldest" is not shown; it has no use for a client (config change, see index dependencies).
  • One cobalt action. There is none on this screen apart from the cobalt word in the heading and the period in the wordmark. Cards use a secondary "View" button on desktop; the filter sheet and date sheet each have a single cobalt apply button when they are open.
  • Tap targets. Chips in the filter sheet and the active filter chips are 44px tall on phones. The date and city inputs are 48px. Page does not scroll sideways at 375px.
  • Names, cities, counts and prices on this page are example data. No stats or response times are shown, because the system does not produce them.
