Design notes
  • Desktop: the calendar goes in the panel, under the price and above the button. It replaces the Start and End fields and the popover from B03, because a calendar that is already on screen does not need two boxes that open another calendar. After a click, the panel shows the chosen dates as a summary line, then the breakdown. This is the B04 behaviour in place: first click sets a single day, a later free day extends it, a range across a busy day gives the B04 message.
  • One cobalt action: the panel button, and only the panel button. The calendar never uses cobalt. Free days are green (success tint and text), unavailable days are muted ink-300 with a hairline strike, and selected days are ink-900 with a faint accent-50 band, exactly the B04 treatment. Before a date is chosen the button reads "Select dates" and moves focus to the first free day (the calendar is already visible, so the button is a guide rather than a gate); after, it reads "Request to book" and goes to checkout. The "Clear dates" and "See April" actions are ink text links, not ghost buttons, so the only accent colour on the screen is the one primary. Hover on a free day is a 1px green keyline.
  • Not colour alone. Free days are also bolder (700) than unavailable days (500), and unavailable days carry the hairline strike used in B04. Today has the small ink dot and is unavailable. The green text on its tint is the same pair as the existing available badge; the Developer should confirm its contrast on the tint at 14px (the text is 700 weight).
  • Notice window. Today and everything inside the model's minimum notice are shown as not available, as briefed. The note under the legend says so in half a sentence, because the notice is already public on the profile. This differs from B04 and B03, where notice is a guide that a client can override. See open question 12.
  • Phone recommendation: a calendar section in the profile body, after "Travel and notice" (notice is context for it) and before Location and reviews. It is one month, 44px cells, with the "Next free" chips above it. Tapping a free date opens the B04 sheet with that start date set; the bottom bar remains the only cobalt action (the sheet has its own, for the same reason B03's sheet does). Why not the others: the strip alone cannot answer "is this model free on my date", and "inside the sheet only" is the status quo, which is the problem being fixed. Cost: about 400px of extra scroll on a profile that is already long, which is why it sits below the facts a client reads first and is not repeated on desktop.
  • Next free dates. Three chips of the nearest free days, shown on phone always and on desktop only when the visible month is empty or sparse, so the panel stays short when the calendar is healthy. One click starts the booking with that date and moves the calendar to its month.
  • Empty month. "No free dates in December. See January" in a hairline box under the grid, with the link going to the next month that has any free date (it names that month, so it never sends a client to another empty one).
  • Honesty. One short line under the legend: "Free dates are from Imogen's calendar, which needs 48 hours' notice. Imogen will confirm your request." Pronoun-neutral, as B03. It sits beside the existing fine print ("You won't be charged yet..."), which already says the model can decline. No claim that a green date is held or guaranteed. Pending requests do not block a date, so two clients can ask for the same day; see open question 14.
  • Model's own view. The same calendar, read-only, with "This is how clients see your calendar" and a secondary "Edit your availability". It is a preview of what clients see; editing is in the wizard's availability step.
  • Engineering. The calendar reads the same availability data as the B04 picker: the model's calendar, existing bookings, and min_booking_notice. Nothing new is stored. The load is the one already needed for the date picker, moved earlier; the page should not block on it (skeleton state). When dates are chosen the panel grows by about 170px; at short viewports give the sticky aside max-height: calc(100vh - 48px) with its own scroll. Phone body calendar tap passes the date to the sheet as the start date. Week starts Monday. This page's script is availability.js; it reuses the booking.js pick and price logic but is not product code.
  • Continuity. The model here is Imogen Hart (shown "Imogen H.", as B03 shows "Ava C."), Manchester, £380. B03 and the rest of the set still use Ava C. at £450; if this is approved, the sample model should be unified in a later pass. Half-day £220 and hourly £50 are made-up sample values.
