Design notes
  • Order follows what a client needs to decide. Portfolio gallery first (swipe on phones with a "1 / 6" counter; a 1 + 4 mosaic with "View all photos" on desktop). Then name, city, rating and categories, then stats, rates, travel and notice, location, reviews, and the "checked" section. The booking panel is always one tap away: sticky on desktop (380px, right), a fixed bottom bar on phones.
  • "About the listing author" is gone (run-through finding 10), along with "Hello, I'm Ava" and the Contact button (open question 7). There is no bio field on a model profile, so a section of repeated name and avatar added nothing. In its place is "Checked before going live": three statements that are true for every visible model today (team review of profile and portfolio, ID verified through Stripe, bank verified for payouts). It stays factual and says nothing about monitoring.
  • Verified signal. No per-card badge (see B01). On the profile the same fact is stated once, as a section, because a client about to hand over a card wants the reassurance at the moment of booking. Public safety and verification claim: confirm with Neil (open question 1).
  • Map removed. The blank grey map box (finding 10) is replaced by a city line with a pin icon. A city-level map shows nothing useful and a pin invites a guess at an address, which we never show.
  • Rates. Day rate is outlined as the one that bookings use. Half-day and hourly are labelled "for reference" because the booking flow only sells whole days; hiding the gap would be dishonest. See open question 2. The fee sentence states the wedge in one line: one flat 15% on top, the model keeps 100%.
  • Minimum notice is shown as a guide, not a rule, because the system does not enforce it (the model can decline any request). If a client picks a start date inside the notice window, a calm, non-blocking line appears (try Tue 3 Nov). See open question 3.
  • Booking panel. The CTA reads "Select dates" until a range exists, then "Request to book". "Book now" is retired: the client is asking, not buying, and every request needs the model's acceptance within 48 hours. The name appears once on the page header and is not repeated in the panel (run-through M12). The panel states above cover logged out, awaiting approval, approved but not ID verified, and a model viewing their own profile.
  • Phone sheet. Tapping "Select dates" opens a full-height sheet with a scrolling four-month calendar (see B04), the live breakdown and the single cobalt action in a sticky footer. The bar's price and the sheet's total always agree.
  • Reviews. Overall rating plus the "Safety & respect" dimension that already exists on the review form. Before the first booking there are no reviews, so the rating line is hidden and the section says so plainly (state shown above). The reviews shown here are example data.
  • Wording is pronoun-neutral ("the day rate", "Ava will travel") because the system holds a gender field but should not infer he or she from it.
  • Tap targets: every date, nav arrow, button and link row is at least 44px tall. The gallery scrolls inside its own container, so the page does not scroll sideways at 375px.
