Design notes
  • The deadline is the first thing under the title. "Reply by Tue 3 Nov, 16:20" with the time left, in the amber of the Requested status. It is a real, system-enforced time (48 hours after the request landed, or the shoot start if sooner), so showing it is honest and creates urgency without nagging. The consequence of missing it is stated once, neutrally: the request expires and the client isn't charged. Nothing says missing it counts against the model, because nothing in the process says that.
  • Earnings are 100% of the rate and shown first on phones. The money is the main decision input, so a phone shows the earnings card before the detail; on desktop it is the first thing in the sticky reply column. The payout wording is the approved promise. The model never sees the 15% as a deduction.
  • Everything the model needs to decide is on one page: dates, shoot type, description, where, the address, the client's note, and who is booking. The client is shown by their verified name and company, which is the same information the shareable shoot summary uses. No client rating or history is shown because none exists to show yet.
  • Accepting is a contract moment. Under the draft licence the model accepts it by accepting the booking (clause 12). The sentence "By accepting, you agree to the Content Licence & Model Release for this booking" sits directly above the button, with the plain-English summary and the full text on the same page. This is a legal surface: wording and whether it is sufficient go to the solicitor (open question 10).
  • Accept is the one cobalt action; Decline is secondary, not red. Declining is a normal, allowed choice, not a danger. A short confirmation says what happens and that no reason is needed, because the process captures none. After accepting, the dates are blocked and the shareable summary appears (state shown above).
  • Private residence. The existing safety note appears above the shoot details, calm in tone, advisory only. The model's own "I don't accept private-residence shoots" setting (profile settings) is applied before a request ever reaches them, silently, so they never see these requests at all.
  • Safety is one quiet link, "Report a safety concern", at the foot of the reply column (SAF-29 exists). It is visible without being alarming, and there is no panic button because the product has none (safety framework v1).
  • Phone: one column, 48px buttons, 44px close and link rows. Order is status, title, deadline, earnings, shoot, who is booking, licence, then the reply card.
