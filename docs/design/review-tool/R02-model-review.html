Checklist and decision
Phone width (375px) · the same page in a 375px frame. Scroll inside it.

Order on a phone. A small summary card first (status, submitted date, both Stripe checks), then the photos, then the profile, then the checklist and the two buttons at the end. A reviewer scrolls the profile the way a client would, then decides without hunting for the buttons.

Bottom bar. Shows how many boxes are ticked and jumps to the decision. It is a secondary button on purpose: the only cobalt button on the page is Approve, once it is enabled.

Tap targets. Every checklist row is at least 44px tall; both decision buttons are 48px.

Design notes
  • The profile is the B03 model-profile layout, unchanged, so the reviewer sees exactly what a client will. The booking panel is swapped for the review panel in the same 380px sticky column. Removed for review: reviews (a pending model has none), the "Checked before going live" section (it would claim a review that has not happened yet), and the booking bar.
  • One cobalt action: Approve. Decline is a secondary (ink keyline) button directly beneath it. Decline is not red: it is a normal, reversible outcome (the person can fix and resubmit with no limit), not a destructive act. Red here would make an honest decline feel like an error and push reviewers towards approving to avoid it. Red stays reserved for real danger, such as a failed save.
  • Approve is disabled until all five boxes are ticked, with a plain line saying why. This makes the checklist a real check instead of decoration. Decline is always available and needs no ticks, because "I looked and it is wrong" is the point. See open question 1.
  • Stripe status never blocks a decision. It matches the approved lifecycle: Gate A (this review) is independent of Gate B (Stripe). The hint under it says what will happen next, so the reviewer is not guessing whether approving makes someone public.
  • Resubmitted shows the previous decline (who, reason, note) inside the panel, so the reviewer can check the specific thing that was wrong instead of starting again.
  • Checklist wording is draft (flag shown on the panel). Item 3 combines nudity and contact details because they are one scan of the photos and text. Note for the PM: the profile has a Links field (website, Instagram). If social handles are banned from "text", that field contradicts item 3, so a rule is needed (see the flags on the index page).
  • Two buttons in the live product link to a decision page, not a pop-up: Decline goes to its own page (R03) so Back works, the preview has room, and it is easy to build. Approve opens a short confirm dialog.
  • Photos are labelled placeholders. Imogen Hart and all details are example data.
